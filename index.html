Case 01 · Embedded Systems Leads

ESP32 Quadcopter Drone Build

The Problem

Building a quadcopter for a university competition, using an ESP32 flight controller. Even sourcing parts was hard — the propellers needed were out of stock in Pakistan and had to be imported from China. Once built, the drone couldn't hold stable speed and balance — motor response was too slow to react to sensor input in real time.

The Fix

Used AI to dig into the motor response problem before touching hardware, working alongside a mentor. Traced the bug to two things: wrong motor direction, and control loop timing that was too slow. AI caught one of the errors in the logic — reviewed and corrected it personally rather than accepting the fix blindly. Then switched propellers from small to mid-range for added mechanical stability.

Validation

Stable speed and balance confirmed across six test flights.

Case 02 · Applied ML — FlyRank Internship

Position Isn't a Gradient: Ranking Signal Analysis

The Question

Content teams commonly assume worse search position means higher review priority, and sort their backlog that way. Tested that assumption directly against real search performance data.

The Finding

Growth rate is flat (~17.5–18.2%) across search positions 1 through 50 — then drops to 3.4% beyond position 50. A cliff, not a gradient. The common assumption doesn't hold within the top 50 ranks.

Model vs. Baseline

MethodPrecisionRecallROC-AUC
Baseline (position > 50 rule)0.9710.0180.508
Gradient boosting model0.8100.9960.737

The baseline looks precise but catches under 2% of at-risk pages. The model trades some precision for catching nearly all of them.
